 # Uzbek Legal Explainer AI (Huquqiy Savodxonlik)

An open-source Legal-Tech project aimed at increasing public legal awareness and legal culture in Uzbekistan by transforming complex statutory laws into accessible, plain-language 2D animated and infographic explainer videos.

## Project Architecture
- `/scripts` — Verified Uzbek legal explainer video scripts based on the Civil, Labor, and Administrative Codes of the Republic of Uzbekistan.
- `/pipeline` — Python & FFmpeg automation scripts for structuring legal slides, generating TTS narration manifests, and rendering educational videos.

## Impact & Goal
Making everyday legal rights (labor disputes, consumer protection, insurance claims, and court procedures) understandable for over 37 million citizens through open educational content on Telegram, Instagram, and YouTube.
